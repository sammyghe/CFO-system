# Answer format — LEAD

Every Finance Desk answer, to a human or an agent, uses Renew Capital's LEAD framework.

```
FINANCE DESK ANSWER — FD-000
Request:   <one sentence, as received>
From:      <who asked>
Status:    ANSWERED | PARTIAL | BLOCKED
Date:      <date>

L — <the finding, and what I recommend. One sentence.>
E — <the driver. Why. The seventh answer, not the first.>
A — <the amount at stake, in UGX or USD, stated explicitly>
D — <the decision needed, from whom, by when. Or: "Nothing needed from you.">

Figures used:  <name, value, as-of, status — every one from canon>
Recorded in:   <where the answer now lives>
```

## The three rules

**1. If you cannot write the A line, you do not yet understand your own number.**
Renew, Session 2. An answer without an amount is a comment.

**2. "Nothing needed from you" is a legitimate D line.** Renew says so explicitly. Use it
when it is true, rather than manufacturing an ask.

**3. Every figure is cited with its status.** Not `USD 400,000` but
`USD 400,000 (APPROVED, as-of 2026-09-21)`. An agent parsing the answer needs the status;
a human reading it needs the confidence.

## For agents

Answers are returned in this block format so they can be parsed. `Status: BLOCKED` means the
request could not be answered and the `E` line says what is missing.
