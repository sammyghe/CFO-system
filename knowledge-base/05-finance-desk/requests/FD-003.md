## FINANCE DESK ANSWER

**L —** Agreed and formalised. UNBS certification is marked **EMBARGOED** in canon. Until the
certificate is verified as filed, no agent and no person may state or imply certification, or
use any impact, quality or volume claim derived from it.

**E —** The brief already reached this conclusion independently and applied it to the
leave-behind. The embargo makes it systematic rather than a judgement call remade each
morning — including by agents that will draft outbound material at 06:00 without a human
reading it first. A certification claim is the single easiest thing to state casually and the
single hardest to walk back with a regulator or a funder.

**A —** Not quantifiable in currency, and that is the point: the downside is credibility, and
credibility has no recovery schedule. Wednesday's room contains Viva con Agua, WASEU, Young
Water Solutions and the Hilton orbit. These are people who fund water quality. A claim that
does not survive one question is worse than no claim.

**D —** Nothing needed from the Capital Brief — it was already doing the right thing. From
Sammy: confirm the certificate is filed and send the reference, and the embargo lifts the same
day. This is GAP-06, and it also carries a schedule risk — a certification delay pushes the
whole ramp right and turns a one-month loss into a one-quarter loss.

## What you CAN say on Wednesday

The plant specification is a fact and needs no certificate to state: ultrafiltration, carbon,
DMI-65, **no reverse osmosis**, TDS 119, minerals retained. That is a technical description of
equipment we own. It is defensible to a chemist and it is more persuasive than a certificate
number to this particular audience.

What you cannot say is that the output is *certified*, or attach any impact figure that
depends on it.
