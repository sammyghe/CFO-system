## FINANCE DESK ANSWER

**L —** The brief's instinct is right and its recommendation is probably right — but I cannot
certify any per-site number today, so the figure is marked **DISPUTED** and is embargoed from
every outbound document until it is decomposed. On Wednesday, the honest answer to "what does
a site cost?" is the honest one.

**E —** These two numbers are almost certainly not disagreeing — they are **measuring
different things**. USD 20,000 reads like the plant-and-install core; USD 80,000 reads like a
fully-loaded site including premises, working capital, jars and launch costs. That is option
C, and structurally I agree with it. But I cannot sign it, because I cannot yet see **what is
inside the 80,000**. The source of truth carries cost of sales and the monthly overhead as
single blended figures — GAP-13 and GAP-16 — and you cannot validate a build-up against a
blend. Picking a number today would mean certifying a figure I have not verified, to funders,
in writing. That is exactly what this desk exists to stop.

**A —** The headline ask is **USD 400,000**, and it is APPROVED at that level. What is
unsafe is dividing it. The exposure is not the arithmetic — it is that eight funders were
contacted on 21 September and Kiva replied within the hour. Two funders receiving two
different per-site numbers is the kind of thing that is never recovered from.

**D —** From Sammy, before Wednesday:

- **Send me the build-up behind the USD 80,000** — the line items, however rough. One page is
  enough. With it I can rule on FD-002 in a day and lift the embargo.
- **Until then, if asked on Wednesday, say this:** *"The 2026 programme is USD 400,000 for
  five locations. We're finalising the per-site build-up this week and I'd rather send you the
  real breakdown than quote a number across a table."*

That is a strong answer, not a weak one. An operator who refuses to guess at his own unit
economics in a room full of funders reads as disciplined. Renew's own rule: *"I'm not
comfortable with that number yet"* shows judgment without pretending certainty.

## What resolves it

Decompose USD 80,000 into two layers and confirm both against real quotes:

| Layer | What it should contain |
|---|---|
| **Core** — plant and install | Ultrafiltration unit, carbon, DMI-65, pumps, tanks, install, commissioning |
| **Fully loaded** — core plus | Premises deposit and renovation · initial jar float · opening stock · licences and certification · launch working capital · contingency |

Then the Wednesday sentence becomes, as the brief put it: *"the plant is twenty, the site is
eighty, five sites is four hundred thousand."* One breath, two layers, both true.
