# Learning and money

## Learning (Sunday 07:00)
1. Rewards come from the policy table: cash 100, committed 60, diligence 30, meeting 15, referral 8, an advancing reply 4, any reply 1, bounce −5, complaint −20.
2. Segment = `tier|lane|route`. Never compare philanthropists against tech investors.
3. `mdam.learning.Learner`: 1 in 5 sends explores. ADOPT or REJECT only once each arm has 15 sends. Three straight failures retire an arm.
4. Each lesson gets a card in the Lessons tab and in the Methods Library format: name, proof (sample size), use it how, status.
5. Score messages (grounding, personalisation, ask clarity, proof, language, compliance) **separately** from outcomes.
6. Refresh any dossier older than 180 days. Produce the top-25 list and the do-not-contact list.

## Money
- The ladder is ASKED → DISCUSSED → PROPOSED → COMMITTED → AVAILABLE → RECEIVED → DEPLOYED → IMPACT_VERIFIED.
- "Sounds interesting", a pledge or a signed agreement with open conditions is **not** cash.
- When someone says money was sent: queue a reconciliation for Sammy. Only `human:<name>` plus a bank-statement reference moves it to RECEIVED (`mdam.ledger`).
- Restricted grants are tracked apart from unrestricted money.
- Never ask for, store or see bank logins.
