# The personal ask

## Shape (100–180 words)
1. **Subject = the ask.** e.g. "Would you help make Maji Safi's US$1 million expansion happen?"
2. **Them first.** One sentence with their sourced hook, including month and year.
3. **Us in one line.** Maji Safi (Safiflow Ventures Group Ltd), Kampala.
4. **One or two proof points**, only `APPROVED` claim ids from `claims.yaml`.
5. **The line:** "I intend to raise this million. Would you like to be the person who makes it happen?"
6. **One small next step:** a 20-minute call, or "may I send the one-page plan?"
7. Sign-off: Samuel Ghedamu · Founder, Maji Safi · Kampala, plus the opt-out line from the policy.

## Builder (B) vs Impact (I): never blend
- **B** (investors, founders, family offices): the plant, daily sales, cheap repeatable sites, local operators, milestones.
- **I** (philanthropists, foundations): families boil water over charcoal or firewood, the smoke harms them, and we already sell certified safe water. Measured people reached.

## Follow-ups and re-asks
- Follow-up 1 and 2: same thread, **one new fact** each, shorter than the first email.
- Close-the-loop: "I'll stop here. If timing changes, I'd love to hear from you."
- Re-ask after 90+ days: name the earlier email honestly, then lead with the **new proof rung**.
- "Later" replies: quote their own timing back to them.

## Banned
Attachments on first contact. The landed machine cost. "coal". "$40/DALY". The CO2 figure without the fNRB caveat. Invented relationships or urgency. Flattery passed off as fact. Any claim not in `claims.yaml`. The Tech Safari banned words (leverage, synergies, solutions, unlock, seamless, robust, disrupt).

Where only Sammy knows a fact, write `[SAMMY: …]`. The gate blocks any draft that still contains one.
