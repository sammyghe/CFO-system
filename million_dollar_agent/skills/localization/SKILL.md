# Localization

## When
Only with public evidence: the person's own posts, website, speeches or interviews in that language. A name or nationality is **not** evidence. Mainland → Simplified (`zh-Hans`). Hong Kong / Taiwan → Traditional (`zh-Hant`). Singapore → check what they publish in. If unsure, use English.

## How
1. Translate the final approved English draft. Keep the names, **US$1,000,000 / 100 万美元**, the instrument and every claim exactly as they are.
2. Back-translate to English and diff it against the original. Any change in meaning → fix it, or fall back to English.
3. The first use of a language template sets its status to `NEEDS_REVIEW` until a fluent human approves it in `claims.yaml`. After approval, personalise inside the template.
4. German/Swiss foundations: sign as **Safiflow Ventures Group Ltd**.
