Ug Life

A Kampala life world you open from a WhatsApp link. You walk the street, make your Ug Tag, earn simulated shillings, run a kiosk and bring your friends. It's built so agents run the daily work and Sammy steers it in about two hours a week.

Everything below follows from these four. With one person on call and no budget, the system has to stop small problems from ever reaching you.

What ships first

One street block in one Kampala neighborhood, drawn in an isometric 2.5D view with realistic lighting, local signage and matatus. It runs on a mid-range Android phone over mobile data, with a first load of 5 MB or less. The server and data are designed so a 3D version can be added later without starting over.

Open link→Make your Ug Tag→Walk the street→Do a delivery shift→Earn UGX (simulated)→Lease a kiosk→Paint your sign→Share card to WhatsApp→Return for tomorrow's street event

Included in v1: Ug Tag with verified socials, privacy toggles and ghost mode; one fictional pothole report-and-fix; block and report. Left out of v1: real money, betting, real brands or officials, voice chat, Nairobi, drivable cars, user code.

The company on paper

It's set up the way a large company would run a game in-house: each function exists on paper with one owner. Here the owners are agents with narrow permissions, and you're the only human. The hard rule is that no agent moves money, bans a player, deletes an account or posts publicly by itself.

Each agent has an off switch, and every action it takes is logged in the repo. A single "maintenance mode" switch pauses sign-ups and all economy activity. Agents run as scheduled Claude Code sessions on the repo, which your existing Claude plan already covers. Player-facing AI chat stays off until revenue pays for it.

What reaches you, and when

P0 · interrupts you now

Safety threat or a report involving a child, a money or ledger exploit, site down for more than 30 minutes, a data leak, a legal notice, or press.

P1 · waits for Monday

Ban appeals, refunds (once real money exists), creator deals, economy changes, broadcast approvals.

P2 · agents handle and log it

FAQs, bug reports, how-to questions, routine moderation. You only see the counts.

Customer care

Channels

In-game Help button, a WhatsApp Business number (free app with quick replies and an away message), and email.

Triage

The support agent sorts each ticket into how-to, bug, account, safety or payment.

How-to

Answered from the FAQ and closed.

Bug

Dev & QA reproduces it and links the fix. The player gets a reply when it ships.

Account

Goes into your Monday queue.

Safety

The content is hidden right away and you get a P0 alert.

Learning

Every answer that wasn't already in the FAQ becomes a new FAQ entry, so support costs less each week.

How it spreads

  • Ug Tag card to WhatsApp StatusEvery shared card carries the join link. Status is where Kampala actually shares things.
  • Daily street eventA short daily moment with a result you can share, like Wordle.
  • ReferralsYou and the friend you invite both get a cosmetic item. Never cash.
  • Real shops claim their kioskShop owners bring their own customers in. This is your free distribution.
  • Weekly Kampala challengeComes with a creator spotlight each week.
  • Campus ambassadorsMakerere, Kyambogo, MUBS, UCU. Paid in status and cosmetics at first.
