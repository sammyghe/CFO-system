**Page 1 · Summary: "Belize already pays for water. Nobody sells it a spring."**
The answer in five lines: the market, the gap, our offer (jars, then cans), what it costs, and what we need from Mike and the landowner.

**Page 2 · The market: "Eight in ten homes buy their drinking water."**
- 74.3% of 110,719 households use bottled or purified water as their main source (2022 census).
- Three money pools: household jars (the biggest, roughly US$16–38M a year, our model), retail single-serve (about US$7–8M, Statista, modelled), and visitor single-serve (about 551,698 overnight visitors in 2025, ~70% from the US).
- Why people buy: trust. The 2025 national survey found E. coli in ~30% of household samples.

**Page 3 · Who is there: "One giant, a long tail, and no spring."**
| Player | Where | What they do |
|---|---|---|
| Crystal (Bowen & Bowen) | 4 plants incl. Ladyville and Esperanza | 11-step treatment with double RO. 500 ml, 5-gal (BZ$6), pouch. Its BZ$40M 2016 bottling facility covered all drinks, not just water. Exclusive Coke bottler. |

- The MoH samples **105** bottled/purified operations every month.
- **The gap:** no named spring brand, no canned still water, and RO everywhere, which strips the minerals out.

**Page 4 · The rules: "What it takes to sell water in Belize."**
- **BZS 10:2002** (compulsory): bottled and packaged water. Covers spring and purified water, treatment, microbiology, packaging and labels. **BZ CP 4:2007**: the hygiene code.
- **Water Industry Act, Cap. 222, Part V (s. 61):** a licence to take water. The National Hydrological Service issues it.
- **Returnable Containers Act, Cap. 328:01:** a deposit on bottles **and cans** up to 1 gallon. Dealers and distributors must take empties back.
- **Plastic rules, SI 17 of 2022:** bans plates, cups, straws and bags. Water bottles are not banned.
- **Fiscal Incentives Act No. 4 of 2023 (BELTRAIDE):** duty relief on plant and machinery, up to 25 years on the regular programme. MSME track: up to BZ$1M of imports, 4 + 4 years. Foreign companies can apply.
- **To confirm with Belize counsel:** Department of Environment clearance, the Companies Act steps, GST and the trade licence.

**Page 5 · Our answer: "Jars carry the business. Cans carry the brand."**
- **Jars first:** the biggest pool, repeat orders every week, and a dispenser that keeps the customer. Price at Crystal parity (BZ$6) and win on spring, proof and delivery. This is the Oasis/Primo model.
- **Cans second:** for resorts, bars, tour boats and visitors. A premium price, and no plastic bottle. The deposit law already covers cans.
- **PET as an option:** on the same combo filler, used only where buyers ask for it.

**Page 6 · The spring and the plant: "We keep the spring a spring."**
- UF + UV removes germs and keeps the natural minerals, with no chemicals added. The water test decides the final modules (Safi Treatment, ten modules).
- One plant at 6,000 L/h feeding two lines: 20L jars, and one can/PET combo filler. All machines under US$250k.
- Our proof: the Kampala plant, UNBS certified, with accredited lab results.
- The limit is the spring's dry-season flow. We need at least 15 L/min measured.
