## Products by player and what sells
- **Crystal is now "Crystal by Dasani"** (Bowen & Bowen rebrand, Ambergris Today, 9 Oct 2026; water unchanged; new look first on 5-gal jars).
- Crystal sizes: 500 ml, 1 L, 1.75 L, 1 gal (bevco.bz), plus the 5-gal jar and the pouch (Breaking Belize News 2020).
- What sells most (estimate; no sales data is published): home 5-gal jars ≈ 162M L a year (82,264 homes × 2 a week × 52 × 18.9 L) carry the volume; 500 ml carries the units (visitors ≈ 2.5–5M L a year); pouches are cheap and facing a phase-out push; cans are imported sparkling only.

## Rules and regulators
- BZS 10:2002 (bottled water, covers spring and purified, compulsory); BZ CP 4:2007 hygiene code. Belize Bureau of Standards.
- Water Industry Act Cap. 222, Part V s.61: abstraction licence.
- Returnable Containers Act Cap. 328:01: deposit on bottles and cans up to 1 gallon.
- SI 17 of 2022 plastics rules (bottles not banned); water pouch phase-out campaign; manufacturers to meet plastic standards by Dec 2026.
- Fiscal Incentives Act No. 4 of 2023 (BELTRAIDE): duty relief on machinery.
- US FDA 21 CFR 165.110: spring water definition (for any US export).

## Social impact facts
- About 20 rural villages lack basic water systems; only 58.3% of schools have drinking water (UNICEF 2024).
- IDB: US$10.64M to bring safe water to 20,000+ households in 44 rural communities; BZ$10M SEAM project (Apr 2026) in Toledo and Corozal villages.
- Plastics were 67% of litter in the 2025 TrashBlitz audit; single-use beverage bottles lead.
- Nearly 40 groups are calling for an end to single-use water pouches.
