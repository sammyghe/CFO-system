A spring is usually Clear or Ground water

Ultrafiltration (0.01 µm) and UV take out germs and keep the natural minerals, with no chemicals added. That keeps a spring a spring.

Five water types, ten Safi Treatment modules · Maji Safi · Belize spring · 3

Every project starts with a raw-water test, and the result sets the treatment. Most springs fall in the first two types, highlighted. Many plants in Belize use reverse osmosis, which strips the minerals out. Our UF and UV approach keeps them, and that is our difference. If the test shows salt, we would still make good water, but it would be sold as purified, not spring.
