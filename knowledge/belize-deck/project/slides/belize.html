Belize is small, under 400,000 people in about 110,700 homes, but it receives over half a million overnight visitors a year and almost a million cruise passengers. About 69% of overnight visitors are American, and around 28% stay in San Pedro on Ambergris Caye. It is English-speaking and the Belize dollar is fixed at two to one US dollar, which makes it easy for US partners and investors.
