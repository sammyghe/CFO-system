SIB 2022 Census · BTB 2025 · San Pedro Sun · Returnable Containers Act, Cap. 328:01 · one plant feeds a jar line and one can/PET filler · 10

Why both. Jars are the business: 82,000 homes already buy water every week, a dispenser keeps them, and the BZ$6 jar sets the price. Cans are the brand: visitors, resorts and bars, no plastic bottle, and Belize’s Returnable Containers Act already puts a deposit on cans. Small PET bottles stay an option on the same filler, for buyers who ask.
