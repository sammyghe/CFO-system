Capacity from population, not money. Of 110,719 homes, about 82,000 buy their drinking water. If we win five in every hundred, that is about 4,100 homes and 1,370 jars a day, roughly 26,000 litres. One 6,000 litres-an-hour plant does that in about four and a half hours. The real limit is the spring: this alone needs about 23 litres a minute, so the dry-season flow measurement sets how far we can go.
