The economics, from public numbers. About 82,000 homes buying two jars a week at BZ$6 is a BZ$51 million a year jar market. Five per cent of it is about 428,000 jars, BZ$2.6 million a year. Five per cent of visitor-days buying one can at about BZ$4 adds about BZ$1 million. Together that is about BZ$3.6 million, US$1.8 million, a year in sales at shelf prices, before costs, from one plant on about half a shift. Mike prepares the set-up cost estimate with us; the spring’s flow decides how far we can grow.
