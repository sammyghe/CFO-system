Maji Safi · Belize spring · 12

Five steps. Mike and the landowner locate and document the spring. Our engineers visit and take lab samples, repeated in both seasons. The landowner’s team logs the flow every week through the dry season. Belize counsel handles the land agreement, the water licence and the bottled water standard BZS 10. Only then do we build, launching jars in one district before adding cans.
