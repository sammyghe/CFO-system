Crystal, owned by Bowen and Bowen, leads: four plants, double reverse osmosis, and the same trucks that carry Coca-Cola. In 2016 Bowen opened a BZ$40 million bottling facility for all its drinks. Behind it are local plants in Belmopan, Corozal, Orange Walk and San Pedro, home RO systems and grocery refill stations. Every one sells purified water. None sells a named spring, and none sells still water in a can.
