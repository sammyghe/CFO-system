The gap: every brand sells purified water. Nobody sells a named spring, or still water in a can.

Coca-Cola logo shown; other tiles show the company name until we have their logo files · company sites and directories · Maji Safi · Belize spring · 7

Crystal, owned by Bowen and Bowen, is the giant: four plants, double reverse osmosis, and the same trucks that deliver Coca-Cola, which Bowen bottles. In 2016 Bowen opened a BZ$40 million bottling facility for all its drinks. Below it is a long tail of local plants, plus home RO systems and grocery refill stations. Every one of them sells purified water. Nobody sells a named spring or still water in a can.
