**For:** Mike only (first read). **From:** Sammy Ghedamu, Safiflow Ventures Group. **Date:** 24 Oct 2026.
**Status:** draft for Sammy to edit. All numbers are a working model, not facts, until the water test and the demand study replace them.

## 1. The idea in one paragraph

A US spring-water brand, with its source on a named spring in Belize. It starts with still water in cans, sold to Belize resorts, bars and visitors, then to US buyers through importers and co-packers. Alongside it, a 20L jar and dispenser service for Belize homes and offices, Oasis/Primo style. Safiflow builds and runs the plant and proves the water. The landowner brings the spring and the land. Mike brings the deal and the network. Investors come in at a US company they can trust, and only as each step is proven.

## 2. Why this can work

- **Belize already buys its water.** 74.3% of households use bottled or purified water as their main drinking water (2022 census, about 82,000 homes). We don't have to create the habit.
- **Trust is the product there.** The 2025 national survey found E. coli in about 3 of every 10 household water samples, mostly from storage and handling. A water that stays sealed until you open it, with a lab report behind it, answers that worry directly.
- **Nobody sells spring water in a can.** The market is one big purified brand (Crystal, owned by the Coke bottler) plus about a hundred small RO plants. We found no named spring source and no canned still water.
- **The visitors are American.** 551,698 overnight visitors in 2025, about 70% from the US, staying about nine days. They already know canned water from home.
- **Plastic is the soft spot.** In the 2025 beach litter audit, plastic bottles were the most common item, and most were the leading brand's.
- **The US brand playbook exists.** Liquid Death started with almost no plant of its own, used US co-packers, and raised its money on brand. We copy that move, with one difference: we own a real source and a real plant.

## 3. What we sell

| Phase | Product | Who buys it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Still spring water, 355–500 ml cans | Resorts, dive lodges, bars, tour boats, premium grocers. Mike's network. |
| 1 | Same water in PET (same filler) | Grocers and events, only where cans don't fit |
| 2 | 20L jars + dispensers, home and office delivery | Belize households and offices, and the landowner's estate |
| 3 | Sparkling, then flavoured | Same buyers, once the core can sells again and again |

Health claims stay off the can. We print what the lab measures, not words like "alkaline" or "purest".

## 4. Why Safiflow runs it

- **We already run a plant.** Our Kampala plant runs every day at about 6,000 L/h and is UNBS certified for packaged drinking water (Oct 2026 to Oct 2027).
- **Independent lab proof.** E. coli, coliforms and Salmonella were all clear in two accredited-lab panels (May and Sept 2026).
- **Our treatment suits a spring.** Ultrafiltration and UV remove germs and keep the natural minerals, with no chemicals added. Plants that use RO strip the minerals out, and that weakens a spring claim. The water test decides the final modules.
- **Low-cost, automated lines.** We source the lines from China and know the suppliers. All machines, imports, set-up and dispensers come in under USD 250,000.
- **We design, build and service.** We send the engineers, train local operators and keep a spares kit on site.

## 5. The plant

- **One treatment plant**, 6,000 L/h (with room to grow to 10,000 L/h), feeding a buffer tank.
- **Line 1:** a 20L jar line (wash, fill, cap) plus dispensers.
- **Line 2:** one combo filler that runs cans and PET on change parts.
- **Staff:** about 8 people at steady state (3 operators, a QA technician, 2 drivers, sales/accounts, a plant manager).
- **What really limits it is the spring, not the machines.** We need a dry-season flow of at least 15 L/min measured. Year-5 demand in our model is about 5–7 L/min on average.

## 8. How we prove demand (what investors read)

1. **Desk sizing:** residents, visitors, hotel and restaurant counts.
2. **Price audit:** shelf and menu prices in 3+ districts and the tourist hubs, with photos.
3. **25–40 buyer interviews:** volume, price, objections. Mike's network opens the first 10.
4. **Concept test:** 100–200 residents and visitors react to the brand boards and a display can.
5. **Commitments table:** letters of intent and pre-orders.
6. **US leg:** one US importer or distributor in writing, and two co-packer quotes.
7. **Regulatory memo:** BZS 10 certification path, abstraction licence, and FDA rules for a foreign spring-water facility.

Output: about 10 pages plus a one-page summary.

## 11. What could break it (named first)

1. **The spring.** Too little dry-season flow, or water that needs RO. Then it isn't a spring story. Gate 0 answers this before real money is spent.
2. **The leader copies us.** Crystal's owner already cans other drinks. Our defence is the named source, lab proof per batch, and a brand built faster than they can.
3. **Aluminium.** We found no recycling chain in Belize, so no "kill plastic" or reef claims until a take-back scheme exists.
4. **The US leg is regulated.** FDA treats imported bottled water as food: facility registration, prior notice for every shipment, and the FDA definition of spring water. All of it can be done, and all of it has to be costed.
5. **Focus.** Kampala still needs its founders. Until Gate 1 passes, Belize runs on Mike, local professionals and a few hours a week from us.
