03
We know our water
The water decides the technology
Uganda
  • Boreholes high in iron and manganese
  • Lake and river water with silt and colour
  • Bacteria, including E. coli
  • Smell and taste complaints
Kenya
  • High fluoride in the Rift Valley
  • Salty boreholes in the north and coast
  • Bacteria in shallow wells
Ethiopia
  • Very high fluoride in the Rift Valley, often 3–18 mg/L
  • Turbid surface water
  • Bacteria at open sources
Every project starts with a raw-water test. The result sets the treatment train.

Pre-treatment takes out metals, colour and smell before the membranes, so they last longer.
