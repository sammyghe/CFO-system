## 1. The project in one paragraph

A Belize partner (a business friend of Mike's) owns a large piece of land with a spring on it. Maji Safi would turn the spring into a Belize water business: **spring water in 5-gallon jars for homes first, then cans for visitors and resorts, with small PET bottles as an option on the same filler.** Maji Safi brings testing, treatment (Safi Treatment systems), the plant and operations. The Belize partner brings the spring and the land; any further role is to be agreed together. An investor joins once the water and the flow are proven. **The aim of the document is one decision: start a joint test of the spring** (site visit, lab tests in the wet and dry seasons, weekly flow log Feb–May).

## 3. Group positioning
- **Safiflow Ventures Group:** parent company, water treatment technology (design, build, install, service). In Belize: technology partner and operator.
- **Maji Safi:** our drinking water brand, produced daily in Kampala (about 6,000 L/h). In Belize: the proof and the jar/delivery model.
- **Safi Treatment:** our system family. 10 modules (M1 micron, M2 iron/Mn, M3 carbon, M4 UF 0.01 µm, M5 UV-C, M6 chlorine, M7 fluoride, M8 NF/RO, M9 cleaning, M10 controller), 500–20,000 L/h. For a spring: M1 → M4 → M5 keeps the natural minerals. RO would make the product "purified", not spring.
- Certification wording: **do NOT say UNBS** in Belize documents. Say "Built to East African Standards: EAS 153 (packaged drinking water), EAS 39 (hygiene), EAS 38 (labelling)". Lab: E. coli and coliforms 0 (accredited lab, May and Sept 2026).

## 6. Economics (market sales, NOT our costs; estimates)
- Home jar market ≈ **BZ$51M a year** = 82,264 homes × 2 jars a week × 52 × BZ$6.
- At a 5% share: ≈ 428,000 jars, 4,100 homes → **BZ$2.6M a year**.
- Cans: 5% of 4.97M visitor-days (551,698 × ~9 nights), one can each at ~BZ$4 → 248,000 cans → **BZ$1.0M a year**.
- Together ≈ **BZ$3.6M ≈ US$1.8M a year** at shelf prices, before costs.
- Capacity: 1,370 jars a day ≈ 26,000 L/day ≈ 4.5 hours of one 6,000 L/h plant. It needs ~23 L/min of spring flow (jars only), so **dry-season flow sets the ceiling**.
- Machines (both lines, landed) were budgeted under US$250k in earlier internal work. Keep investment and cost figures OUT of the partner documents; Mike prepares the estimate.
