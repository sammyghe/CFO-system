Here is what makes Belize different. About 84% of homes have public piped water, yet 74% drink bottled or purified water. People don’t trust what reaches the glass. The 2025 national survey found E. coli in about 30% of samples where water is collected and 34% where it is drunk, mostly from storage and handling, and it was found in dispenser water too. So the product that wins is not only clean water but a clean, sealed service.
