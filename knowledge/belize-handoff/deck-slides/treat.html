For a spring we expect the lightest line: micron filters, ultrafiltration and UV, then filling. That removes germs and particles and keeps the natural minerals, which is what makes it spring water. Iron, carbon, fluoride or salt modules are added only if the lab test shows the need. Salt removal means the product becomes purified water, not spring.
