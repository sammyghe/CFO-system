What makes spring water different. Under the US FDA rule, spring water must come from an underground formation that flows naturally to the surface, the spring’s location must be identified, and if a borehole is used it must tap the same formation and give water of the same composition. Belize’s standard BZS 10 also covers spring water. In the US, spring water built the largest branded water company, Primo Brands, with US$6.66 billion of net sales in 2025 through regional spring brands like Poland Spring and Arrowhead. Bottled water is America’s number one drink by volume, and most of Belize’s visitors are American.
