Prices per litre, from the cheapest to the dearest. The Crystal 5-gallon jar is BZ$6, about 32 cents a litre, and that is what households pay every week. A can of imported sparkling water costs about BZ$4 on Ambergris, more than eleven dollars a litre. Coca-Cola’s 12 oz glass at one dollar is the price everyone knows.
