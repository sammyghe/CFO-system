The aim

The aim is one decision: to start a joint test of the spring, so every later choice rests on facts

The first step proposed: a site visit by Maji Safi engineers, lab tests in the wet and dry seasons, and a weekly flow log through the dry season (February to May). If the water and the flow hold up, the partners decide on the build.

Maji Safi · Belize spring waterExecutive summary
Executive summary

Belize already pays for its drinking water. No one sells it a spring.

#FindingWhat it means
1The habit exists74.3% of homes drink bottled or purified water, even though 83.8% have public piped water. Demand doesn’t have to be created.
2Trust is the gapThe 2025 national survey found E. coli in 29.8% of samples at collection and 33.7% where water is drunk, mostly from storage and handling. Sealed, tested water with a clean dispenser service answers it.
3The market is all purifiedOne national leader (Crystal, Bowen & Bowen) and a tail of local RO plants. No named spring water and no still water in cans were found.
4Visitors bring spring habits551,698 overnight visitors in 2025, 69% from the US, where bottled water is the No. 1 drink and regional spring brands built the largest bottled water company.
5Size is set by the springServing 5% of buying homes takes about half a shift of one 6,000 L/h plant. The limit is dry-season flow, so testing comes first.
Proposed approach: spring water in 5-gallon jars with dispenser delivery first, cans for resorts and visitors second, small PET as an option on the same filler. Start with a joint test of the spring.
Sources: SIB 2022 Census; SIB/MoHW/BWS MICS7 statement, Nov 2025; BTB preliminary 2025; IBWA citing BMC; Primo Brands 2025 Annual Report3

Maji Safi · Belize spring water4 · The approach
4 · The approach

Jars carry the business, cans carry the brand, and one light treatment line serves both

First · Spring water jars

5-gallon jars with a dispenser and fixed-day delivery for homes and offices. 82,000 homes already buy weekly. Priced at the BZ$6 people know.

Second · Cans

Still spring water for resorts, bars, tour boats and visitors. No plastic bottle; the Returnable Containers Act already covers cans.

Option · Small PET

On the same filler as the cans, only where buyers ask for it.

Exhibit 9 · Safi Treatment line expected for a spring (final modules set by the lab test)

Added only if the test shows the need: M2 iron and manganese, M3 carbon, M7 fluoride. M8 salt membranes would make the product purified, not spring. Always on: M9 cleaning and M10 Smart Controller. No chemicals in the drinking water. Built to East African Standards (EAS 153, 39, 38); accredited lab tests show zero E. coli at our Kampala plant.

Source: Safi Treatment Technical Data Sheet SF-TDS-001; Chemiphar reports May and Sept 20269
