1 · Summary

Belize already pays for its water. Nobody sells it a spring.

Jars carry the business. Cans carry the brand.

Eight in ten Belize homes buy their drinking water, mostly as 5-gallon jars from one national brand and a long tail of small RO plants. None of them sells a named spring. We enter with spring water in jars, plus a dispenser and delivery. We then add cans for resorts and visitors, and keep small PET bottles as an option on the same filler.

  1. The demand is already there. 74.3% of households use bottled or purified water as their main drinking water, about 82,000 homes.
  2. Trust decides the sale. A 2025 national survey found E. coli in about 3 of every 10 household samples, mostly from storage and handling. People pay for water they can trust.
  3. The market is RO-purified water everywhere. RO removes the minerals. A spring treated with UF and UV keeps them, and that is our difference.
  4. Size comes from the spring, not the machine. One 6,000 L/h plant can serve several percent of the national jar market in a single shift (section 8). What we must prove is the dry-season flow.
  5. Nothing is built until the water is tested. Section 9 lists the ten steps, starting with a site visit and lab samples.

Three pools of demand

  • Household jars, the biggest and most regular: about 82,000 homes buying 1.5–3 jars a week.
  • Visitor single-serve: about 5 million visitor-nights a year, concentrated in Ambergris Caye, Cayo and Belize City (56% of overnight stays in 2023).
  • Retail single-serve for residents: shops, gas stations, schools.

What people want

  • Water they trust, sealed until opened.
  • A jar that always arrives, never just topped up. Crystal jars ran out in 2020 and a dead frog was found in one in 2024.
  • Less plastic. In the 2025 beach litter audit, plastic bottles were the top item.

6 · Our offer

How we win: the Oasis and Primo model, with a real spring

Primo and Oasis both grew on the same habit: a dispenser in the home and a full jar on a fixed day. We bring that model to a market that already buys jars.

First · Jars

Spring water at home and at the office

  • 5-gallon (18.9 L) jars, the size Belize already uses, at Crystal's price.
  • Dispenser placed with the first order. Jar swapped one for one.
  • Order on WhatsApp, delivered on a fixed day by zone.
  • Every jar comes back to the plant, is washed and refilled, and sealed. Never just topped up.
  • Batch lab results behind a QR code on the seal.
Second · Cans

The brand people see

  • Still spring water in aluminium cans for resorts, lodges, bars, tour boats and premium grocers.
  • The deposit law already covers cans, so take-back can be built with distributors.
  • Mike's network opens the first resorts.
  • Small PET bottles on the same filler, only when a buyer asks.

Claims we keep off the label

  • Health claims ("alkaline", "cures").
  • "Purest", "safest", or any comparison with Crystal.
  • "Plastic-free" (can linings are plastic) and reef claims until a can take-back runs.
  • "Spring", until the lab and BZS 10 confirm it.
7 · Treatment

Before the test: what the spring might need

The water decides the technology. Much of inland Belize sits on limestone, so expect hard water and quick changes in clarity after heavy rain. Tick what the test might show to see which Safi Treatment modules would run.

The tests we need

  • On-site screen at the first visit: TDS, conductivity, pH, temperature, turbidity, and a field E. coli kit.
  • Accredited lab panel against BZS 10: E. coli, total coliforms, enterococci, Pseudomonas; hardness, calcium, magnesium, sodium, potassium, bicarbonate, chloride, sulphate, nitrate, nitrite, fluoride, iron, manganese, arsenic, lead and other metals.
  • Farm chemicals if there are fields or orchards upstream.
  • Repeat in both seasons: wet (about Jun–Nov) and dry (about Feb–May).
  • Flow logging every week through the dry season: the lowest number sets the plant size.
  • Sanitary survey of the catchment: livestock, septic tanks, roads, farming.

Why our treatment fits a spring

  • UF at 0.01 µm stops bacteria and particles and keeps the natural minerals.
  • UV is the final barrier before filling. No chemicals go into the water.
  • RO only if the water is salty, and then the product is sold as purified, not spring.
  • Proven daily at our UNBS-certified Kampala plant, with E. coli and coliforms at 0 in accredited lab panels (May and Sept 2026).

Module numbers follow the Safi Treatment Technical Data Sheet SF-TDS-001.

9 · Steps

Ten steps from a spring on a map to a jar on a table

Each step passes before the next one starts. Steps 2 to 6 run side by side.

  1. 1
    Mike + landowner

    Locate and document

    District and nearest town, GPS pin, land title, photos and video of the spring, any past water test or well log.

  2. 2
    Maji Safi engineers

    Site visit and first samples

    On-site screen, sterile lab samples, a sanitary survey of the catchment, the plant site, power and road access.

  3. 3
    Landowner's team, our method

    Log the flow through the dry season

    Weekly flow readings from now to the end of May 2027. The lowest reading sets the plant size.

  4. 4
    Accredited lab

    Lab panels, wet and dry

    The full BZS 10 panel in both seasons. This decides the treatment modules and whether the spring claim holds.

  5. 5
    Belize counsel

    Rights and permits

    Landowner agreement, abstraction licence, environmental screening, company set-up, BELTRAIDE application.

  6. 6
    Mike's network + our team

    Market audit

    Shelf and menu prices in Belize City, San Pedro, Belmopan, Placencia and Cayo, with photos. 25–40 buyer interviews. Letters of intent from homes, offices and resorts.

  7. 7
    Maji Safi engineers

    Design the plant

    Safi Treatment modules chosen from the lab results. A jar line plus one can/PET combo filler, sized to the flow and the letters of intent.

  8. 8
    Bureau of Standards, Ministry of Health

    Build, commission, certify

    Install, train local operators, run the commissioning test, then certify against BZS 10 and BZ CP 4.

  9. 9
    Operating company

    Launch jars in one district

    Dispensers, WhatsApp orders, fixed delivery days. Prove that homes reorder before adding the next district.

  10. 10
    Operating company + Mike

    Add cans, then PET if asked

    Resorts and visitor areas first, with can take-back through distributors.
