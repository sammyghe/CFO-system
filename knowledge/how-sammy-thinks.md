# How Sammy thinks and works (knowledge base)

Read this before any task for Sammy. Add to it after every session.

## Thinking style
- **Big target, plain words.** "One million dollars, 1,000 lives, one man's dream." He sets the outcome and wants the system to chase it relentlessly.
- **Results = money in the account.** Not contacts, not emails, not replies. Report progress on that ladder.
- **Systems over one-offs.** He wants machines that learn: score, test, retire and keep what works ("agent loop to learn and get better").
- **Go to the top first.** Billionaires, then US$100M+, then down. Direct, personal, by email.
- **Persistence with a reason.** "Come back and ask again". But each return should bring something new.
- **Global reach.** He's happy to write to someone in their own language (e.g. Chinese) when it fits.
- **Builds on what exists.** He shows prior work (the Money Desk) and expects the next version to beat it, not replace it.
- **Autonomy.** He wants agents to act (send, follow up) without him approving every step, inside clear rules.
- **Reuse free tools.** Open-source GitHub tools and agent swarms or teams, "recruited" as needed.

## Working with him
- Start by confirming the task is about Maji Safi (his standing preference).
- Give him the decision, not a survey. Recommend; flag the few choices only he can make.
- Short sentences. Tech Safari voice for anything published (see the `maji-safi-voice` skill).
- His time is the bottleneck: the Money Desk always has 9–10 items waiting on him. Put his moves first, keep them to 3 a day, and pre-draft everything.
- Never invent a fact for him to sign. Use `[SAMMY: …]` placeholders.
- Side projects and GitHub: he wants designs he can take into his own repos (this one: sammyghe/cfo-system, CrewAI + Google Sheets).
